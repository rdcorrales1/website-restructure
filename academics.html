PISAY Academics

PISAY provides a six-year Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) education focused on research, innovation, and academic excellence.

Programs

Junior High School

Grades 7 to 10 develop strong foundations in mathematics and science, along with communication, technology, and research skills.

Senior High School

Grades 11 and 12 provide advanced STEM coursework that prepares students for university study and careers in science and technology.

Research Program

Students learn scientific investigation, project development, data analysis, and technical communication through research courses and projects.
